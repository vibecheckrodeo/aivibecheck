Why I’m doing Vibe Check

Proposed byline: Ashley Raiteri

If you’re building with a coding agent and you’re stuck, I want to look at the project with you.

You’ve got something running. Then the login stops working after deployment, or a change breaks a part of the app you weren’t touching. You ask the agent to fix it. It changes a few things. You’re still stuck, and now you have more code to understand.

That’s the sort of problem I want you to bring to Vibe Check.

I’m Ashley Raiteri. I’m a serial CTO, and I’ve been programming since 1980. I’ve spent my career building, securing, hosting, and fixing software. I can sit down with you, look through what you have, and explain what I’d investigate or change.

The project matters. A description can tell me that login is broken. The code, the deployed app, and the error message give us something to work with. I may need to see what happens before I can tell you whether I’m the right person to help.

I’m particularly interested in helping people who want to keep building with their coding agent. We can look at the assumptions behind its changes, check what is actually happening, and work out a useful next request. Sometimes that request will be to change code. Sometimes it will be to collect evidence before changing anything else.

I’m also using coding agents on this site. My first version of the booking section was mostly a large price and a payment button. Looking at it again, I realized it was asking you to pay before I knew who you were or what you needed. I wanted the page to start with your project, so I changed the order.

You register with your name and email, describe the project and the problem in 1,000 words or less, and share what I need to see. A few sentences are fine. GitHub, Lovable, Replit, Claude, Figma, a live URL: send the material that helps me understand the problem. Use public or read-only access, and keep passwords and secrets out of the request.

I review your request before asking you to pay. Once I’ve approved it and we’ve agreed on the focus, you pay the $25 deposit and choose a time within the next week. Reviews are $25 per 15 minutes, on Zoom, Monday through Saturday, 1–6pm Eastern. The scope is fixed and there are no refunds. We agree on any extra time before extending.

If you haven’t paid the deposit within seven days of completing registration and sharing the project, we remove our access to what you shared. That doesn’t delete your project. For Figma, we delete our stored access and refresh tokens; you should also revoke the app in your Figma account. Separate invitations need to be removed in the service where you granted them.

A short call has limits. We may find that the problem needs more investigation. Implementation, a full audit, and security certification aren’t included. I’ll explain what I can establish from the material and time we have, and what I’d check next.

If that would help, tell me what you’re working on.
