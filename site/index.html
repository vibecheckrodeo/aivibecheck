    What will we do on the call?

    We’ll look at the problem you brought. I’ll explain what I find, what I’d change or check next, and what I’d ask your coding agent to do. The review doesn’t include implementation or a written report.

    Do I pay before you’ve looked at my request?

    No. Register, describe the problem, and share the project first. I’ll review your request before asking for the $25 deposit.

    What does the deposit cover?

    It buys an answer or a 15-minute conversation about your project. I’ll reply before the call with my estimate of how much time we’ll need. Choosing more time is up to you.

    What happens to the access I share?

    At the unpaid deadline, scheduled cleanup clears saved request content from the active app and starts removing connected access I control. Separate invitations need removal in that provider; I track them until verified. Payment records, audit events, unresolved removal tasks, provider copies, and Cloudflare recovery history may remain.

    Can we go longer?

    Yes. Reserve 30 minutes for $45 total (10% off), or an hour for $80 total (20% off). Your $25 deposit counts toward that price, so you pay another $20 or $55. Extra time must be available and paid for before the call. Upgrade payments are non-refundable when I can deliver the extra time. I’ll spend the time you book with you.

    Do you sign NDAs?

    There isn’t a standing NDA. Mention your requirement in the request before sharing confidential material. Don’t send passwords or production secrets.
