In plain English

The sign-in got back to the app, but the app rejected it.

The nonce is a one-time value that links the reply to the original sign-in attempt. Here, it was left out when the app exchanged the sign-in code for tokens.

The suggested change is to pass that same server-stored value through. Keep the checks that verify the token’s signature.

That gives us something specific to check in the code, then test with a real sign-in.

    What does the deposit cover?

    What happens to the access I share?
